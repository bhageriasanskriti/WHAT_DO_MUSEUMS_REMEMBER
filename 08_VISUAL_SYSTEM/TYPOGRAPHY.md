# Typography

## Roles

### Display
Elegant editorial serif for:
- hero question;
- chapter titles;
- large dates;
- key statements.

### Accent
Very limited italic/script/calligraphic voice for:
- small editorial interventions;
- selected annotations.

Never use it for essential information.

### Information
Highly readable serif or sans-serif for:
- metadata;
- descriptions;
- controls;
- statistics;
- source links.

## Typographic behaviour

Large numbers should be treated as visual objects, not dashboard KPIs.

Use:
- oversized numerals;
- changing scale;
- tracking;
- line breaks;
- labels anchored to object clusters.

Final font selection should be tested for:
- Latin diacritics;
- long museum titles;
- mobile wrapping;
- readability at small metadata sizes.
