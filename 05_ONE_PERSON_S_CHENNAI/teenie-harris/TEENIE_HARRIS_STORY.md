# Teenie Harris Story

## Role in the exhibition

The Teenie Harris Archive is the strongest supplied dataset for a chapter about how one lens can create a record of community life.

## Validated quantitative identity

- 59,031 records.
- 59,031 classified as `Photographic Negative`.
- 59,031 image URLs supplied.
- 59,031 specific archive URLs supplied.
- 59,031 creator fields populated in the supplied table, overwhelmingly Charles “Teenie” Harris.

## Interaction concept

### Opening
A dense contact sheet.

### Scroll
The sheet changes by time window and selected descriptive keyword.

### Focus
One negative enlarges.

### Story
Its supplied title becomes the primary caption-like text.

### Return
The image re-enters the contact sheet and its neighbours become visible.

## Research questions

- What becomes visible when a collection is read through captions?
- How does one photographer's archive change the scale of “collection”?
